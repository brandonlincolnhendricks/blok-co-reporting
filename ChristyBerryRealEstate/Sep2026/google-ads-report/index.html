BANNER ADS

Banner ads - your name reached about 107,500 people for $243

Banners keep your name in front of people as they browse websites and apps, so we judge them on being seen, not on leads. This month your banners were shown 229,395 times to about 107,500 people, about 2.1 times each, against about 35,600 people last month.

Shown: 3 of the 12 sizes of this design. Across all 12 sizes this month: 213,421 views, 325 clicks.
  • Cost: every 1,000 views cost $1.06, down from $3.83 last month, so reaching 1,000 different people cost about $2, against about $6.
  • On screen: 67% (153,954 of 229,381 measured views) were actually on screen, up from 53% last month.
  • Spend: $243 this month, up from $217 last month, with the daily budget currently set at $8.00.
  • Designs: Live Luxuriously was shown the most (213,421 views, 325 clicks), and Dallas Luxury Lives Here was shown 15,974 times, with 150 clicks.

What this means for you

Think of a banner as a billboard: judge it by how many people see it and whether it is actually on screen. This month your banners reached more people, and more of their views were on screen, than last month.

CLICKS AND VISITS

Clicks and visits - 475 banner clicks and 789 visits to your website

Banner clicks fell to 475 from 568 last month, while the banners were shown far more often (229,395 views against 56,733), so the click rate fell to 0.2% from 1.0%. Each click cost $0.51 on average, against $0.38. We treat banner clicks as a light signal.

  • 431 of 475 banner clicks came from phones, and 97.9% of banner views were on mobile.
  • ESPN had 161 clicks from its views, while The Weather Channel had 235 clicks from far fewer views.
  • Your website's visitor numbers credit 789 visits this month to your Google ads, close to last month's 797 and well above the 266 of a usual month. Visits and clicks are counted separately, so they do not match.

What this means for you

Visits to your website held steady from last month. We will keep an eye on the click rate in the coming month.

Plain-English glossary
Reach
How many different people were shown your banners, counting each person once. It is Google's estimate, and months cannot be added together.
Views on screen
A view is a time a banner was shown on a page. It counts as on screen when at least half the ad was on screen for at least one second.
Cost per 1,000 views
What it cost to show your banners 1,000 times.
Click rate
The share of banner views that led to a click, 0.2% this month.
Lead
An ad visitor who tapped your phone number on your website, signed up on your home search, or sent a form such as a contact, showing or home-value request.
A usual month
The average month across June to August. It keeps an unusually busy or quiet month from setting the bar.
