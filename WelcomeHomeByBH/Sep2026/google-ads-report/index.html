SEARCH ADS

Search ads - 345 clicks at $2.91 each, up from 341

Search ads appear when someone types what they want into Google. Your Nashville Furnished Rental Search ads drew 345 clicks for $1,003, up from 341 last month and about 340 in a usual month, and each click cost $2.91, against $2.95 last month.

  • Your ad showed 2,645 times, fewer than the 3,414 times last month and 3,053 in a usual month.
  • People clicked 13.0% of the times it showed, against 10.0% last month and 11.1% in a usual month, so fewer showings still brought slightly more clicks.
  • Spend held steady at $1,003, against $1,005 last month and $1,004 in a usual month.

What this means for you

Your ad is clicked more often when it shows, which kept clicks steady even as it showed less often. Click costs also eased a little.

AD RANK

Room to grow - your ad showed on 17% of the searches it qualified for

Google counts every search your ad could have appeared on. Your ad showed on 17% of them, the same as last month. It missed 67% because its ad rank was too low, against 71% last month, and 16% because the day's budget ran out.

  • Google sets ad rank from the bid and from how useful the ad and its page look, so the ad's wording and the page it opens are the next things to improve.
  • We are not recommending a bigger budget. The aim is to win more of the searches the current $33 a day can already reach.

What this means for you

Ad rank is the main thing keeping your ad off more searches. Improving it is our next focus, though Google decides each search, so we cannot promise a change.

Plain-English glossary
Lead
An ad visitor who sent a property info request or contact request, signed up on your home search, or tapped your phone number on your website. A phone tap shows the tap, not whether a call followed. Counts can include parts of a lead, because Google sometimes shares credit for a lead across more than one ad click.
Home-search sign-up
A visitor created an account on the listings search on your website, usually to save a search or get listing alerts. An early signal, not a request to talk.
Click rate
The share of the times your ad showed on Google that someone clicked it.
Cost per lead
Your ad spend divided by your leads.
A usual month
The average of June, July and August. It keeps one unusually busy or quiet month from setting the bar.
Ad rank
Google's score for each ad in each search, set from the bid and from how useful the ad and its page look. Ads with too low a rank do not show.
