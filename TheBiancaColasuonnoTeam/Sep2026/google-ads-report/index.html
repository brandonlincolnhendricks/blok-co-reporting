SEARCH ADS

Astoria Home Search - 1,907 clicks and 115 leads

Search ads appear when someone types what they want into Google. Your Astoria home-search ads drew 1,907 clicks for $1,007, up from 1,473 last month and above a usual month's 1,450, at $0.53 per click. They brought 115 leads, against 82 last month and 73 in a usual month, at $8.75 per lead, against $9.34 last month.

  • Lead mix: 114 website sign-ups and 1 call from the ad's call button.
  • Click rate: 9.5% of the times the ads showed led to a click, against 10.3% last month and 8.3% in a usual month.
  • The ads showed on 18% of the searches they qualified for. They missed 68% because they ranked too low, down from 76% last month, and 14% because the day's budget ran out.
  • Where clicks came from: Google could place only some of this campaign's clicks, and of the 279 it placed, 16 came from people in Astoria. The campaign is set to show in Astoria, Jackson Heights, Long Island City, Woodside and Greenpoint.

What this means for you

This campaign brought most of your leads this month at a lower cost per lead. The next thing to improve is how often its ads rank high enough to show; better ad wording and pages can help, though Google sets the rank.

BANNER ADS

Banner ads - your name reached about 60,700 people for $242

Banners keep your name in front of people in Queens as they browse websites and apps, so we judge them on being seen, not on leads. Your banners reached about 60,700 people (Google's estimate), against about 37,400 last month and about 25,700 in a usual month.

Shown: 3 of the 12 sizes of this design. Across all 12 sizes this month: 107,325 views, 192 clicks.
  • Views: shown 111,932 times, about 1.8 times each, and 75% of the views Google could measure were on screen (84,123 of 111,854 measured views), against 73% last month and 74% in a usual month.
  • Cost: $2.16 per 1,000 views, against $4.15 per 1,000 views last month and $4.86 per 1,000 views in a usual month, or about $4 per 1,000 people reached against about $7 last month.
  • Clicks: 215, against 213 last month and 271 in a usual month, a click rate of 0.2%. 189 of 215 banner clicks came from phones, so we treat them as a light signal. No leads came from banners this month, and that is not how we judge them.
  • Your static design was shown 107,325 times for 192 clicks, and the dynamic design 4,607 times for 23 clicks.

What this means for you

Think of the banners as signs along the streets people in Queens use every day. This month about the same spend put your name in front of more people, at a lower cost per view.

SEARCH ADS

Your Queens home-search ads drew 322 clicks for $488, up from 300 last month but below a usual month's 366. They brought 11 leads, 8 website sign-ups and 3 calls from the ad's call button, against 16 last month and 14 to 15 in a usual month, at $44 per lead, against $30 last month and $34 in a usual month.

Plain-English glossary
Lead
An ad visitor who took a contact step the account counts: a website sign-up or form, a call from an ad's call button, or a tap on your phone number. Taps on your Google Business Profile listing are counted separately.
Website sign-up
Recorded as YLOPO Registration: a visitor submitted a form or reached a thank-you page on your website. It shows interest; it does not confirm a conversation.
Reach
How many different people were shown your banners in the month, counting each person once. It is Google's estimate, and it cannot be added across months or campaigns.
On screen
A banner view counts as on screen when a large enough part of the ad was visible for long enough, by Google's standard. A banner low on a page nobody scrolls to does not count.
Cost per 1,000 views
What it cost to show your banners 1,000 times.
A usual month
The average of the months from June to August. It keeps one unusually busy or quiet month from setting the bar.
