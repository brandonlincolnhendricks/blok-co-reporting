LOCAL SERVICES

Local Services ads - 6 phone leads for $697, with clicks at $4.68

Your Local Services ads show at the top of Google results and were the only campaign to record leads this month. They brought 6 phone leads for $697, $116 per lead, against 7 leads for $768 last month and 12 to 13 leads for $1,348 in a usual month.

  • Showings and clicks: the ads were shown 1,270 times and clicked 149 times, against 1,409 and 157 last month and 1,627 and 210 in a usual month.
  • Cost and click rate: each click cost $4.68, against $4.89 last month and $6.42 in a usual month, and 11.7% of showings led to a click, up from 11.1% last month, against 12.9% in a usual month.
  • Clicks to leads: 4.0% of clicks became a lead, against 4.5% last month and 6.0% in a usual month. 1 message also came through the ads, counted apart from leads.
  • Phone leads kept coming after the month closed, most recently on October 6 (not counted here), and 46 have come in since June 1.

What this means for you

Clicks got cheaper, but the ads were shown less often and fewer clicks became leads. The 6 here are what Google Ads recorded; your Local Services lead list is the full record and may show more.

NEIGHBORHOOD MATCH

Neighborhood Match - 821 clicks at $0.56 each, no recorded lead

Neighborhood Match is a Performance Max campaign, which shows your ads across Google, and we report it as one campaign. It drew 821 clicks for $457, against 851 last month and 790 in a usual month, and each click cost $0.56, down from $0.67 last month and $0.75 in a usual month.

  • Showings: 73,147, against 80,298 last month and 76,552 in a usual month, and 1.1% of showings led to a click, the same as last month.
  • Leads: none this month and none recorded since June 1. The account counts a Neighborhood Match Request as a lead.
  • Interest on your Google listing tied to this campaign: 57 other taps such as photos and reviews, against 26 last month and 57 in a usual month, and 11 taps through to your website. These are not leads.

What this means for you

These ads bring low-cost clicks to your website, but none has turned into a recorded request since June 1. A quick test request will show whether requests are being recorded.

A lead is someone who reached out after an ad, counted from the lead actions the account tracks. This month all 6 were phone leads through your Local Services ads. Across the account, each lead cost $268, against $266 last month and $206 in a usual month.

HOME VALUE

Home Value - 185 clicks, down from 300, no recorded lead

Home Value is also a Performance Max campaign. It drew 185 clicks for $451, against 300 last month and 288 in a usual month, and each click cost $2.44, up from $1.75 last month and $2.01 in a usual month.

  • Showings: 50,571, against 71,600 last month and 88,225 in a usual month, and 0.4% of showings led to a click, the same as last month.
  • Leads: none this month and none recorded since June 1. The account counts a Home Valuation Completion as a lead.
  • Interest on your Google listing tied to this campaign: 10 taps through to your website and 3 other taps. These are not leads.

What this means for you

Fewer showings and higher click costs this month. Before changing the ads, we want to confirm that a home-value request on your website is recorded.

SPEND AND CLICKS

Spend - $1,605 this month, against $2,606 in a usual month

Your ads drew 1,155 clicks for $1,605, against 1,309 clicks for $1,859 last month and 1,366 clicks for $2,606 in a usual month. All of it went to Local Services, Neighborhood Match and Home Value.

  • Local Services spent $697, against $1,348 in a usual month. Neighborhood Match ($457) and Home Value ($451) each spent less than in a usual month.
  • Your YouTube video and Demand Gen campaigns, including Wellington Reserve, showed no ads and spent $0.00 this month. Wellington Reserve spent $0.49 last month.
  • The chart shows spend, clicks and leads day by day. Leads are dated by the day of the ad click.

What this means for you

Most of the gap with a usual month was on Local Services, where spend, showings and leads were all lower.

Plain-English glossary
Lead
Someone who reached out after an ad, counted by the lead actions your account tracks. This month every lead was a phone lead through your Local Services ads. Taps on your Google listing are counted separately.
Local Services phone lead
A phone call that came through your Local Services ad, which Google charges as a lead.
Performance Max
A Google campaign type that shows your ads across Google. We report each one as a whole.
Showings
Times an ad was shown. Also called impressions.
Click rate
Out of all the times an ad was shown, the share that led to a click.
A usual month
The average of June, July and August. It keeps one unusually busy or quiet month from setting the bar.
