BANNER ADS

Banner ads - your name reached about 8,700 people for $72

Banners keep your name in front of people as they read the news, so we judge them on being seen, not on leads. Shown here is your most-seen design, which invites people to picture a move to Maple County.

Banner headline

“Thinking of a Move to Maple County?”

Shown: 3 of the 12 sizes of this design. Across all 12 sizes this week: 5,045 views, 44 clicks.
  • Reach: your banners were shown 11,867 times to about 8,700 people, about 1.4 times each, and about 3 in 4 views (8,821) were actually on screen.
  • Cost: every 1,000 views cost $6.03, against $4.40 in a usual week, so the same $10 a day reached about 8,700 people instead of about 12,000. Your biggest site cost more: The New York Times charged about $7 per 1,000 views, against about $5 the week before.
  • Clicks: 74, against about 95 in a usual week, because there were fewer views; the click rate held at about 6 per 1,000 views. 68 of the 74 came from phones, and most of the visits that followed lasted under five seconds, so we treat banner clicks as a light signal. Two banner clicks were followed by a tap on your phone number.

What this means for you

Think of a banner as a billboard on the commuter route out of the city: judge it by how many people it reaches and whether it is actually seen. Leads are a bonus.

SELLER ADS

Seller ads - 29 clicks for $61; every Google search click came from Maple County

Your seller ads drew 29 clicks for $61. The 19 that came from Google searches were all from people in Maple County, as the campaign is set up to do. The other 10 came from small ads Google placed on other websites. In all, 16 of the 23 website visits started on one of your home-value pages, and most of the rest on Elmwood and Oak Ridge listings pages.

  • No lead came from your seller ads this week. Seller requests come in now and then: six since June, the latest a market-report request on August 17. The last home-value request was July 16.
  • For every 100 searches your seller ad could have shown on, it showed on about 13. It missed 73 because its ad rank was too low to show, and only 14 because the day's budget ran out. Google sets ad rank from the bid and from how useful the ad and its page look.
  • Your August market report gives sellers a reason to ask: it shows the county's single-family median price up from a year earlier.

What this means for you

These ads reach the right area on Google search. Our recommendation is a sharper home-value ad, within the current budget (recommendation 4).

Quick check

Search words

Google shows the search words behind 5 of your 6 home-search leads, and 3 of those were Harborview searches such as "duplex for sale harborview". Once we hear from you about last week's city sign-ups, we recommend screening out searches for homes in the city itself, such as "condos for sale harborview", a few at a time, and stopping if phone taps fall. This may mean fewer leads at first. People in the city who search for Maple County homes stay in. Separately, we recommend a test within the same budget: searches for homes in Millbrook, Glen Haven, Elmwood or Brookside would open your new guide to that town, now live, instead of the listings page. Plain-English glossary
Reach
How many different people were shown your banners, counting each person once. It is Google's estimate, so treat it as approximate.
Views and on screen
A view is one time a banner was shown on a page. It counts as on screen when at least half the ad was visible for at least one second; a banner low on a page nobody scrolls to does not count.
A usual week
The average of the four weeks before this one, August 17 to September 13. It keeps one unusually busy or quiet week from setting the bar.
Click rate
Out of every 1,000 times an ad was shown, how many times someone clicked it.
Lead
An ad visitor who tapped your phone number on your website, signed up on your home search, or sent a form such as a contact, showing or home-value request. A phone tap shows the tap, not whether a call followed. Taps on your Google Business Profile are counted separately.
Home-search sign-up
A visitor created an account on the listings search on your website, usually to save homes or get new-listing alerts. An early buyer signal, not a request to talk.
