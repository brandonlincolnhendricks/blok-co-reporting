The month in 30 seconds

Your Nashville Home Search ads drew 197 clicks for $486, the same number of clicks as a usual month, and none of the searches Google shows were for your team's name. AD RANK

Ad rank - your ad missed 82% of eligible searches by ranking too low

Your ad showed on under 10% of the searches it could have shown on. It missed 82% because its ad rank was too low, against 72% last month and 77% across June to August, and 13% because the day's budget ran out, against 22% last month. Google sets ad rank from the bid and from how useful the ad and its landing page look.

  • Click rate: when your ad did show, 6.9% of people clicked, up from 6.4% last month and close to the usual 7.1%.
  • Times shown: 2,847, against 3,183 last month and 2,784 in a usual month.
  • Click cost: $2.47 per click, against $2.38 last month and $2.30 in a usual month. Over a longer stretch, clicks cost $2.36 on average in the last 3 months against $1.82 in the 9 months before, so the same budget buys fewer of them: about 206 clicks a month instead of 242.

What this means for you

The biggest room to grow is ad rank, not budget, and we are not recommending a bigger budget. Sharper ad wording that matches the towns people search is the next thing we would work on, and we will report what moves rather than promise a ranking change.

What we recommend next

Recommendations for the coming month

1

1. Test new ad wording built on the towns people search

Ad wording test

We recommend testing new ad wording that names the towns people search most, such as Franklin, Lebanon and Brentwood, and checking that each ad opens a home search for the town it names, within the current $16 a day. The aim is a better ad rank on the searches your ad currently misses; we will report what changes rather than promise a result.

2

2. Screen out searches for other firms' names

Search words

We recommend screening out searches for other real estate firms and agents by name, a few at a time. This month they took 9 clicks for $15 and 5 clicks for $11, with no leads, and screening them out leaves more of the same budget for people shopping for homes.

3

3. Confirm where the ads should show

Targeting

We recommend reviewing the campaign's area list with you. It is set to show in Orange County, Los Angeles County, Santa Barbara County, Ventura County, Cook County, DuPage County, Lake County, New York City and the San Francisco Bay Area as well as Davidson County, Wilson County and Williamson County, while most clicks came from people in Davidson County, Wilson County and Williamson County. If your team does not serve the other areas, we would remove them, and we will not change anything without your go-ahead.

4

4. Keep watching home‑search sign‑ups

Keep watching

We recommend no change here yet. Sign-ups were last recorded on October 3; if the quiet stretch runs past the 8 days seen in August, we will make one quick test sign-up to confirm new sign-ups are still being counted.

Plain-English glossary
Lead
An ad visitor who signed up on your home search, sent a form such as a contact request, or called from the ad's call button. Taps on your Google listing are not counted.
Home-search sign-up
A visitor created an account on the listings search on your website, usually to save homes or get new-listing alerts. An early buyer signal, not a request to talk.
Click rate
The share of times an ad was shown that ended in a click.
A usual month
The average of June, July and August 2026. It keeps one unusually busy or quiet month from setting the bar.
Ad rank
Google's score for whether your ad shows on a search. It comes from the bid and from how useful the ad and its landing page look.
Searches your ad showed on
Out of all the searches your ad was eligible for, the share where it actually appeared. Google gives only a range when the share is very low.
