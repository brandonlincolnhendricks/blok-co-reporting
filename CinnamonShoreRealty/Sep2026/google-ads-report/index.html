CLICKS AND COST

Clicks - 1,960 for $1,203, as each click cost more

You spent $1,203, about the same as last month's $1,216. Clicks cost more on both campaigns, so the same spend bought 1,960 clicks, against 2,434 last month and 2,647 in a usual month.

  • Cinnamon Shore Awareness: each click cost $0.62 on average, against $0.52 last month and $0.48 in a usual month.

  • Of all clicks, 8.1% became a lead, against 9.1% last month and 10.2% in a usual month.
  • Your ads sent 1,991 visits to your website, against 2,536 last month. Visits are counted on your website and clicks in Google Ads, so the numbers differ.

What this means for you

Your budget spent as planned; it simply bought fewer clicks this month. We will keep watching click costs in the coming month before suggesting any change.

Plain-English glossary
Lead
Someone who, after clicking your ad, called from the ad's call button, tapped the phone number on your website, signed up on your home search or sent a contact form. Email-list sign-ups are kept separate.
Call from an ad
A call placed straight from the call button on your Google ad.
Phone tap
A tap on the phone number on your website from a phone. It shows someone meant to call, not whether a call connected or how long it lasted.
Home-search sign-up
A visitor created an account on the listings search on your website, usually to save homes or get new-listing alerts. An early buyer signal, not a request to talk.
Click rate
The share of times an ad showed in search results that someone clicked it.
A usual month
The average of June, July and August. It keeps one unusually busy or quiet month from setting the bar.
