SEARCH ADS

Penthouses search ads - 329 clicks, up from 8 last month

Search ads appear when someone types what they want into Google, so we judge them on clicks from the right searches and on leads. Your Penthouses ads showed 7,254 times this month, up from 222 last month, and drew 329 clicks for $562.

  • Click rate: 4.5%, up from 3.6% last month.
  • Cost: each click cost $1.71 on average, down from $2.29 last month.
  • Every click this month came from the Penthouses campaign; your Miami Home Search ads did not show this month or last month.

What this means for you

Your search ads were seen far more often this month and each click cost less. The next step is making sure those clicks can turn into leads, starting with a quick test.

MISSED SEARCHES

Missed searches - your ads showed on 12% of the searches they qualified for

Google estimates how many searches your ads could have shown on. This month your ads showed on 12% of them, against 28% last month.

  • Ranked too low: 67% of qualifying searches went to other ads because your ad ranked too low, against 71% last month.
  • Budget ran out: 22% were missed because the day's budget ran out, against 1% last month. The campaign is currently set to $25 a day.
  • Google sets ad rank from the bid and from how useful the ad and its page look.

What this means for you

Ad rank is the bigger gap, and it is the one we can work on within the current budget, with sharper ad wording. We are not recommending a bigger budget; any budget change is your decision.

LEADS

Leads - none recorded this month or last month

A lead is an ad visitor who reached out or signed up: a tap on your phone number, a call from the ad, a home-search sign-up, or a form such as a contact, showing or home-value request. No leads were recorded this month, from 329 clicks and $562 of spend, and none last month.

No leads came in from the ads this month.
  • Penthouses: no lead recorded since June 1.
  • Miami Home Search: 5 home-search sign-ups since June 1, the latest on June 26. These ads did not show this month or last month; in a usual month they drew 161 clicks and 1 to 2 sign-ups for $316.
  • Leads are counted from the actions your account tracks, such as phone clicks, contact requests, showing requests and home-search sign-ups. An email sent straight to your team is not counted.

What this means for you

A quiet stretch does not tell us on its own whether fewer people reached out or whether new leads are not being recorded. A quick test sign-up will show which, and each answer points to a different fix.

Plain-English glossary
Lead
An ad visitor who tapped your phone number, called from the ad, signed up on your home search, or sent a form such as a contact, showing or home-value request. Taps on your Google listing, such as directions, are not counted.
Home-search sign-up
A visitor created an account on the listings search on your website, usually to save homes or get new-listing alerts. An early buyer signal, not a request to talk.
Click rate
The share of the times an ad showed in search results that ended in a click.
Share of searches shown
Of the searches your ad qualified for, the share where it actually appeared. It is Google's estimate.
Ad rank
How Google decides which ads show and in what order, based on the bid and on how useful the ad and its page look.
A usual month
The average month from June to August, so one unusually busy or quiet month does not set the bar.
