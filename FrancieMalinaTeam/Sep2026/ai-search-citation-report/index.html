Ask Google “What are the Bronxville property taxes” and its answer is built on your new Bronxville property tax guide, with your team named beside nearly every line. Ask “Which is better to live Irvington vs Tarrytown” or “Which is better to live Rye vs Scarsdale” and your comparison page is the first source Google lists, with ChatGPT and Perplexity citing the same page.

The Rivertowns guides you asked for are already sources. Google’s AI Overviews cite your site on twenty-eight searches, ChatGPT on thirteen and Perplexity on twenty-three, through twenty-six different pages on franciemalina.com.

Google Ads

On September 28 your home search ads moved to the larger daily budget you approved, with the sellers campaign paused to fund it. From September 28 to October 2 the ads brought 16 leads: 11 taps to call your team and 5 home search registrations on your site, against 5 leads in the five days before. Home search ad clicks nearly doubled, from 109 to 201, each click cost less than before, and the cost of each lead fell from about $74 to about $29. Five days is an early read; the full read of the change comes at the end of October.

Google Search

From September 23 to 30, Google Search sent 185 clicks to your site, up from 128 in the eight days before, and showed your site in its results more than 19,000 times, up from about 13,000. Close to half of the extra clicks went straight to the new September pages, led by the Tarrytown and Sleepy Hollow guide, and the rest went to guides already on your site, led by the Bronxville village guide. Across the month, the new September pages appeared in Google Search 2,949 times and earned 31 clicks within days of going live.

Your website

Visits from your Google ads rose from 135 to 211 in the first five days of the change, and 203 of them were engaged visits. ChatGPT sent 34 visits to your site in September, up from 19 in August, and 31 of them were engaged visits, so the people arriving from AI answers stay to read.

Interpretation: The pattern is reach turning into contact. AI answers cite your guides, Google Search is already showing the new ones, and since the budget moved into home search your ads are producing calls and registrations at a lower cost each. October’s report reads the first full month of the change.
