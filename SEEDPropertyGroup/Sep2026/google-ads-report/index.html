SEARCH REACH

Search reach - your ads showed on 14% of the searches they qualified for

Your ads showed on 14% of the searches they qualified for, against 15% last month. They missed 69% because their ad rank was too low, down from 73% last month, and 17% because the day's budget ran out, against 12% last month. Google sets ad rank from the bid and from how useful the ad and the page it opens look.

  • Missed because of ad rank: 69%, against 73% last month and 73% over June to August.
  • Missed because the day's budget ran out: 17%, against 12% last month and 14% over June to August.
  • Shown: 14%, against 15% last month and 13% over June to August.

What this means for you

Ad rank is the larger gap, not budget, and we are not recommending a bigger budget. The next thing to improve is ad rank, by testing new ad wording within the current budget; how Google ranks the new ads is not something we can promise.

BANNER ADS

Banner ads - your name reached about 56,700 people for $243

Banners keep your name in front of people while they read the news or check sports scores, so we judge them on being seen, not on leads. Google estimates about 56,700 different people saw them this month, up from about 37,600 last month and about 26,900 in a usual month, for the same $243 as last month. Shown here is your most-shown banner, Authentic Austin Charm, seen 14,462 times in its wide size.

Shown: 3 of the 11 sizes of this design. Across all 11 sizes this month: 34,752 views, 82 clicks.
  • Reach: your banners were shown 112,002 times, about 2.0 times each, and 78% of the views Google could measure (87,074 of 111,904) were on screen, against 73% last month and 71% in a usual month.
  • Cost: every 1,000 views cost $2.17, against $3.32 last month and $3.17 in a usual month, so the same spend bought more views. Reaching 1,000 different people cost about $4, against about $6 last month.
  • Clicks: 347, against 166 last month, a click rate of 0.3%. 280 of 347 banner clicks came from phones, so we treat banner clicks as a light signal.
  • Designs: 'Dynamic' was shown most (56,574 views), then 'Authentic Austin Charm' (34,752 views) and 'Lets Grow Something' (20,676 views).

What this means for you

Think of a banner as a billboard on the drive into Austin: judge it by how many people it reaches and whether it is actually seen. This month it reached more people for the same spend, and leads are a bonus, not its scorecard.

Plain-English glossary
Reach
How many different people were shown your banners in the month, counting each person once. It is Google's estimate, so treat it as approximate, and it cannot be added across months.
Views on screen
A view is each time a banner was shown on a page. It counts as on screen when Google confirms the ad was actually visible, not just loaded low on a page nobody scrolled to.
Cost per 1,000 views
What it cost to show your banners 1,000 times. Lower means the same spend puts your name in front of more people.
Click rate
Out of all the times an ad was shown, the share that led to a click.
Lead
An ad visitor who signed up on your home search, tapped your phone number or sent a form such as a contact, showing or home-value request. A home-search sign-up means a visitor created an account to save homes or get new-listing alerts: an early buyer signal, not a request to talk.
A usual month
The average month from June to August 2026, so a busy or quiet month on its own does not set the bar.
