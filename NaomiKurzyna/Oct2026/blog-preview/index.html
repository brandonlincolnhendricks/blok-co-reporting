Buying a Home in Philadelphia
A buyer's guide to Philadelphia from pre-approval to settlement. Philadelphia is a block by block and, for condominiums, a building by building market, so a sale starts with pricing and preparation rather than a sign in the window. Here is how I approach pricing, preparation, condo and co-op documents, transfer tax, seller disclosure and your estimated net proceeds.

Living at Waterfront Square, Philadelphia
Waterfront Square is a gated condominium community of three towers on the Delaware River at 901 N. Penn Street. This guide covers where it sits, how two units in the same building can differ, and the questions to settle before you buy or sell there.

A condo and a co-op in the same Center City block can look identical and be owned in completely different ways. This guide walks through the ownership difference, the Pennsylvania resale certificate and your review window, and how to read an association's budget, reserves, insurance, rules and parking before you commit.

A Philadelphia rowhome shares walls, a roofline and often a century of history with its neighbors. This guide covers what to inspect, from the flat roof to the sewer lateral, and how to check permits, historic status, a tax abatement and parking before you commit.
